⚠️ Emerging Issues — Trending Negative Signals
🔴 Packaging Failure Driving Brand Defection
Repeat packaging failures (broken seals, leaking nozzles, crushed boxes) are appearing in 40%+ of negative reviews — this is not a one-off QC issue but a systemic supplier problem actively eroding NPS.
Evidence: CrunchCo resealable zip broken "every single bag"; BrewBright bottle leaked in bag twice; EcoFresh spray nozzle broke after 2 weeks.
🔴 Shrinkflation Backlash Accelerating
Customers are explicitly calling out simultaneous price increases and size reductions — a combination that triggers distrust and active search for alternatives, not just complaint.
Evidence: FreshSip: "Price jumped 30% and can size got smaller — double hit." EcoFresh: "Price increased again, third time this year."
🔴 Stockout-Induced Permanent Churn
Stockout complaints consistently end with customers stating they switched brands and found adequate alternatives — suggesting recovery window is 2–3 weeks before churn becomes permanent.
Evidence: BrewBright cold brew OOS for a month: "already found an alternative brand." EcoFresh OOS 2 months: "had to switch to non-eco brand."

Key Design Decisions
Why TF-IDF + GPT, not pure embedding search?
TF-IDF is fast, interpretable, and requires no vector DB at POC stage. In production we'd layer in semantic embeddings for cross-lingual and paraphrase recall. Hybrid retrieval gives best precision at scale.

Why structured JSON output enforcement?
Fortune 500 clients need deterministic downstream integrations (Salesforce fields, Power BI datasets). Enforcing JSON schema at the prompt layer eliminates post-processing failures and makes the pipeline auditable.

What's the data privacy posture?
All customer PII is stripped at ingestion. Reviews are pseudonymized before storage. Azure OpenAI is used in-tenant so data never leaves the client's environment. GDPR/CCPA compliant by architecture.
